SYSTEM INFORMATION

System Pipeline

End-to-end investigation stages executed for every SAR upload — from image ingestion to forensic source attribution.

  1. 01
    Input

    SAR Detection

    Detects potential oil-spill signatures from Synthetic Aperture Radar imagery. The model identifies dark anomalies consistent with surface oil films.

    ↳ Spill mask · Detection confidence

  3. 02
    Geometry

    Spill Geometry

    Determines the approximate shape, area, and spatial characteristics of the detected spill by fitting a polygon to the segmentation mask.

    ↳ Area (km²) · Polygon boundary · Length (nm)

  5. 03
    Estimation

    Oil Quantity Estimate

    Estimates the approximate quantity of oil based on the detected spill area and an assumed film thickness range. Results are a bounded estimate, not an exact measurement.

    ↳ Volume range (m³) · Mass range (tonnes) · Thickness assumption (µm)

  7. 04
    Reconstruction

    Origin Reconstruction

    Uses Lagrangian drift back-propagation from the spill's observed location to reconstruct the probable source point and discharge time window.

    ↳ Origin lat/lng · Uncertainty radius (km) · Discharge window

  9. 05
    Correlation

    AIS Correlation

    Cross-references the reconstructed origin with vessel trajectory data. Identifies vessels that were spatially and temporally proximate to the discharge window.

    ↳ Candidate vessel list · AIS gap flags · Trajectory overlap

  11. 06
    Scoring

    Candidate Ranking

    Scores each candidate vessel using a composite metric: proximity to origin, trajectory alignment, AIS signal gaps, and speed anomalies. Vessels are ranked highest-to-lowest.

    ↳ Suspicion score (0–100) · Risk level · Score breakdown

  13. 07
    Forecast

    Drift / Forecast

    Uses wind and surface current vectors to project the spill's predicted position and spread at +6 hours from SAR acquisition time.

    ↳ +6 h predicted polygon · Drift path waypoints

Data & Assumptions

AIS source: Synthetic demonstration data — vessel positions and tracks are simulated and do not represent real maritime traffic.

Oil quantity is an estimate based on assumed film thickness. Actual volume may differ depending on emulsification, weathering, and sub-surface components.
